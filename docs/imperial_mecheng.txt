The MEng Mechanical Engineering programme at Imperial College London
runs for four years and combines core mechanical engineering subjects
with mathematics, mechatronics, and professional engineering skills.

First-year modules typically include Mathematics and Computing,
Mechanics, Materials, Fluid Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Design and
Manufacture, and Stress Analysis. Students are graded using Imperial's
percentage-based system, with 70% and above generally corresponding to
First-Class Honours.

Students on the MEng track have the option to pursue a Professional
Engineering Skills module, which develops communication, teamwork, and
project management skills alongside the technical curriculum.
