SAP Joule is an AI copilot embedded across SAP S/4HANA. In sourcing and procurement, Joule agents can read incoming requests for quotation and supplier contracts, extract key terms such as price, delivery dates and payment conditions, and compare them against company policy. Instead of several buyers reviewing every document line by line, the agent flags only the deviations that need a human decision, so a single manager can approve the result. This reduces manual review time and makes the evaluation of large volumes of RFQs and contracts more consistent.
